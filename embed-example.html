The artwork above is generated in the browser with 3x3 pixel blocks, capped at 200,000 cells — the cap coarsens the blocks on a wide viewport rather than letting the frame cost grow with it. There is no image, no video and no request — just bitmotion.js, about 15.6KB over the wire once your server gzips it.

It holds still for prefers-reduced-motion and stops rendering entirely once it scrolls out of view, so it costs nothing while someone reads the rest of the page. At this setting a frame costs around 5ms, so even on hardware several times slower than a laptop it stays well clear of the 50ms long-task threshold.
